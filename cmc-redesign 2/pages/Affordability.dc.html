How this is worked out, and the sources
  1. Lender ceiling. Cayman lenders test a total debt-service ratio: all loan payments, including the new mortgage and the home's running costs, as a share of gross income. 40% is a common guideline; banks set their own figure and may use a different one for non-residents. The ceiling is an input above so you can match your bank's.
  2. Loan from payment. The room left for a mortgage payment is converted to a loan with the standard amortising formula at the shared rate and term, the same arithmetic as the mortgage calculator.
  3. Stamp duty on the transfer. 7.5% of the price (or market value, if higher); Caymanian first-property buyers may qualify for 0% or 3.75% within the bands set out in the Ministry of Finance concession form; detail in the stamp duty guide. Source: Application for Stamp Duty Concession, Caymanian property buyers (v16.4.26).
  4. Stamp duty on the mortgage. 1% of the sum secured up to CI$300,000; 1.5% of the whole sum above that. Source: Stamp Duty Act (2019 Revision), Schedule: Mortgage.
  5. Interest rate default. KYD prime 7.00%, raised from 6.75% on 18 September 2026 (Cayman National notices, checked 1 October 2026); the default of 7.75% assumes prime plus 0.75%. Enter the rate your bank quoted.
  6. Fee estimates (legal, valuation, bank fee) are planning allowances, not quotes.

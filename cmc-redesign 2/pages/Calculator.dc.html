How the numbers are worked out, and the sources
  1. Monthly payment: standard amortising formula on the loan, rate and term entered; extra payments are applied to principal each month.
  2. Stamp duty on the transfer: 7.5% of the price or market value, whichever is higher; Caymanian concessions by buyer type and property type from the Ministry of Finance concession form (v16.4.26). Detail and conditions in the stamp duty guide.
  3. Stamp duty on the mortgage: 1% of the sum secured up to CI$300,000; 1.5% of the whole sum above. Stamp Duty Act (2019 Revision), Schedule: Mortgage.
  4. Interest rate default: KYD prime 7.00% (Cayman National notices, checked 1 October 2026) plus 0.75%.
  5. Lender ceiling: 40% total debt service, a common Cayman guideline; each bank sets its own.
  6. Legal, valuation, bank fee, insurance, maintenance: planning allowances (legal 0.5-1% of price plus disbursements; valuation about CI$600; bank fee about 1% of loan; building insurance about 1% of price a year; maintenance 0.5-1% a year), replaced by your quotes in "Fee assumptions" and "Your household in detail". No published Cayman tariff exists for these.
  7. Currency: CI$1 = US$1.20, the fixed peg.

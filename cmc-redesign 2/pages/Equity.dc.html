Equity calculator

Your equity is what your home is worth minus what you still owe. See it, your loan-to-value, and roughly how much a lender might let you borrow against it.

Recalculate
Assumptions Equity = home value − mortgage balance − other secured loans. Available to borrow = (value × maximum LTV) − everything already secured. Lenders also test affordability, so treat it as a ceiling. Valuation, legal and stamp duty costs on a new facility are not included; see the refinancing guide. A new charge is stamped at 1% / 1.5% of the sum secured under the Stamp Duty Act (2019 Revision). The 80% maximum loan-to-value is a typical Cayman lender cap for a primary residence, not a rule; change it to match your bank.

What moves your equity
  • Every repayment: the principal part of each month's payment lowers the balance. Early in a term that part is small; it grows every year.
  • The market: a higher valuation lifts equity without you paying a dollar; a lower one erodes it.
  • Overpayments: a lump sum off the balance converts straight into equity.
  • Borrowing against it: a home-equity loan turns equity back into debt; the bar above moves left.

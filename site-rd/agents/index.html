How it works

Three steps, no paperwork beyond a yes

1 You say yes One email confirming we may show your listings, and which ones: all of them, a district, or a hand-picked few.
2 We pull the details Price, address, beds, baths, size and one photo from your listing page or a feed you send us. We add the estimated monthly and your agency name.
3 Buyers click through to you Every card links to the listing on your own site. Enquiries, viewings and offers happen there, with you, never through us.

What you get

  • Qualified traffic. People who have just run the numbers on a price like yours.
  • Your name on every card, and the click goes to your page, not a lead form.
  • No fee, no contract, no exclusivity. Ask and a listing comes down the same day.
  • Nothing presented as yours that isn't. The monthly figure is labelled as this site's estimate.

What we ask of you

  • Written permission (an email is enough) naming the listings or the rule ("all our Grand Cayman sales").
  • A way to keep it current: a listings page we can check, a feed, or a weekly note of what has sold.
  • A logo (SVG or PNG) if you'd like it on the card, and the exact agency name to show.
  • Tell us when a listing goes. Sold or withdrawn listings are removed as soon as we hear.

Questions agents ask

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no listing fee, no lead fee and no commission. The site is free for buyers and free for agents.

Do you collect buyer details and pass them on?

No. The site has no forms and no accounts. A buyer who wants to enquire clicks through to your page and contacts you directly.

Where does the monthly figure come from?

From the site's calculator at a stated deposit, rate and term (shown under the grid), and it is labelled as our estimate. It is never presented as your figure or a lender's quote.

Can I pull out later?

Any time, for any reason, by email. Your listings are removed and your logo comes off the page.

Get in touch

Cayman Mortgage Calculator is an independent, free site run from Grand Cayman. It is not a lender, a broker or an agency, and every figure on it is an estimate. One inbox, no forms, no mailing list.

Agents and agencies

Want your listings on the site? This is how it works:

  • Permission first. Nothing is shown until you say yes, in writing.
  • Your page gets the click. Every card links back to the listing on your own site, with your agency named.
  • No fee, no contract. Say the word and a listing comes down.
  • Our estimate, clearly labelled. The monthly figure on a card is this site's calculation, never presented as yours.

Before you write: three things we get asked most

Are the estimates advice? No. They use the standard amortising formula and the figures you enter. Your lender's rate, fees and insurance will differ. Treat every number as a starting point, not a quote.
Can you arrange my mortgage? No. The site doesn't take applications, refer you to lenders or earn commission. Take your numbers to a bank or a licensed broker.
Where do I confirm stamp duty? With the Ministry of Finance (MOF@GOV.KY) and the forms on caymanlandinfo.ky. Our stamp duty guide links to both; the bands change with the budget cycle.

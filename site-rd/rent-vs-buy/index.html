Rent or buy in Cayman?

Buying costs more at the start and less later. This works out the year the two cross over, for the length of time you actually plan to stay.

Renting
Buying
How long you stay
CI$0

Over the years you stay

Buying is compared on what you are left with: the equity in the property after selling costs, less everything you paid in. Renting is compared on what you paid and the deposit you still have. Neither side assumes you invest the difference.

    The rate on the whole value once the price reaches CI$2,000,000

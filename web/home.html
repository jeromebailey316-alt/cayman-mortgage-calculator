The rules behind the numbers

  • Transfer stamp duty: 7.5% of the dutiable value, or 10% of the whole value once it reaches CI$2,000,000.
  • First-time Caymanian buyers pay nothing up to CI$550,000 on a home, then 3.75% on the next CI$100,000.
  • Mortgage stamp duty: 1% of the loan up to CI$300,000, 1.5% of the whole loan above that.
  • There is no annual property tax in the Cayman Islands.

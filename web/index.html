Rules built in

  • Transfer stamp duty: 7.5% of the dutiable value, or 10% of the whole value once it reaches CI$2,000,000 (from 1 January 2026).
  • First-time Caymanian buyers: nothing up to CI$550,000 on a home (CI$250,000 on land), then 3.75% on the next CI$100,000. Joint purchases by 2–10 Caymanians: CI$600,000 (CI$450,000 land). Above the ceiling the concession is lost and 7.5% applies to the full value.
  • Caymanian second property: 3.75% of the full value up to CI$600,000 on a home (CI$300,000 land); jointly CI$700,000 (CI$550,000 land).
  • Mortgage stamp duty: 1% of the loan up to CI$300,000, 1.5% of the whole loan above that.
  • Land Registry: CI$50 to register the transfer and CI$50 to register the charge.

Worth knowing

  • Stamp duty is due within 45 days of signing, or interest accrues.
  • Concessions need an approved application to Lands & Survey. A five-year restriction goes on the register and duty is clawed back pro rata if you sell early.
  • Banks commonly lend 85–90% on a primary home, about 65% on a rental and about 50% on raw land, usually over a shorter term.
  • Fees, insurance and the interest rate here are planning defaults. Swap in your own quotes as they arrive.
  • Listings are copied from the agencies' public websites and refreshed every four hours. A property may have sold or changed price since, so always check the agent's page.
